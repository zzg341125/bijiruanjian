Weave Office Android
====================

This repository is a fork of Weave Office (https://github.com/Jamailar/Weave-Office), MIT,
pinned at f3054c51677cacab7e4ba945d50f7a45b2fd6ae2.

The Android port in apps/weave-office-android/ is an unofficial community port, not made or
supported by the Weave Office developers.

It follows the ArtCraft Android set's five-layer adaptation stack, whose architecture model is
morte2437/photocraft-android (https://github.com/morte2437/photocraft-android, MIT OR Apache-2.0)
and whose build scripts are adapted from kanaaka/shared-android-port
(https://github.com/kanaaka/shared-android-port, MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 kanaaka).

The vendored rfd (https://github.com/PolyMeilex/rfd, MIT) and arboard
(https://github.com/1Password/arboard, MIT OR Apache-2.0) copies add Android stubs only; their
upstream code is unchanged.

No artwork from any of these projects is included: the launcher icon is Weave Office's own
(assets/app-icon/weave-office-256.png).
